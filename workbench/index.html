Technical notes

Polymerase chain reaction (PCR)

PCR amplifies a DNA region using primers and repeated synthesis cycles. RNA targets require conversion to cDNA before conventional DNA amplification.

Professional and academic context: During my placement at La Princesa, I worked with PCR and RNA extraction.

Further reading: NHGRI · Polymerase Chain ReactionEvidence / context

Enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA)

Antibody binding and an enzyme-linked readout support detection or quantification of an analyte. Controls and assay format determine how signals can be interpreted.

Professional and academic context: I worked with ELISA during my immunology placement at La Princesa.

Further reading: Thermo Fisher · ELISA overviewEvidence / context

Western blot

Proteins are separated, transferred to a membrane and detected using antibodies. Blocking reduces unwanted binding; controls support interpretation.

Professional and academic context: My work at La Princesa included gel casting, transfer and blocking.

Further reading: Thermo Fisher · Protein analysis guideEvidence / context

Cell culture and aseptic work

Cell culture maintains cells under controlled conditions. Medium, cell identity, contamination control and culture history influence downstream observations.

Professional and academic context: At La Princesa I worked with HaCaT cells, media preparation, passaging and freezing.

Further reading: ATCC · Primary Cell Culture GuideEvidence / context

Thermogravimetric analysis (TGA)

TGA follows changes in sample mass during a controlled temperature programme. Mass-loss regions can reflect several processes and require context for interpretation.

Professional and academic context: I used the TGA Q500 for biomass analysis during my placement at Celignis.

Further reading: IUPAC Gold Book · thermogravimetryEvidence / context

Elemental analysis (CHNS/O)

Combustion-based CHNS methods quantify carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen and sulfur through analysis of reaction gases. Oxygen determination depends on the analytical configuration.

Professional and academic context: I worked with CHNOS elemental analysis during my placement at Celignis.

Further reading: Natural History Museum · CHNS elemental analysisEvidence / context

Environmental microbiological sampling

Settle plates collect particles deposited by gravity; contact plates and swabs support surface sampling. Results depend on the sampling method and recovery conditions.

Professional and academic context: I performed these methods during academic environmental and personnel monitoring exercises.

Further reading: CDC · Environmental sampling

Viable microbial counts

CFU estimate organisms able to grow under the conditions of the method. Dilution, analysed volume and plate selection affect the calculation.

Professional and academic context: Academic exercises with water, soil and food samples.

Further reading: FDA · Aerobic plate count

Metrology and traceability

Metrological traceability connects a measurement result to a reference through a documented calibration chain. Classroom checks teach the method, deviations and limitations.

Professional and academic context: I completed thermometer calibration and pH measurement exercises with calculations and records.

Further reading: NIST · Metrological traceability

Agarose gel electrophoresis

DNA fragments separate by migration through a gel. A size marker supports estimation of fragment lengths; absent bands or smearing call for review of sample quality and preparation.

Professional and academic context: Group exercise using genomic DNA and PCR products, in addition to my placement at La Princesa.

Further reading: NHGRI · Electrophoresis

Scientific reading notes

Bacteriophage therapy

Phages infect bacteria; therapeutic approaches investigate selected lytic phages. Host specificity, bacterial resistance and clinical evidence remain central challenges.

Professional and academic context: This topic relates to my co-authored academic literature review.

Further reading: Bacteriophage Therapy: Developments and DirectionsEvidence / context

Immunology: cells and soluble signals

Immune-cell populations and soluble mediators provide complementary information. Cell isolation and immunoassays answer different questions; neither alone establishes a clinical diagnosis.

Professional and academic context: This note connects the theory with my practical experience of CD4/CD14 isolation and ELISA.

Further reading: Thermo Fisher · ELISA overviewEvidence / context

Molecular virology: viral entry

Viruses use host-cell components to enter cells; routes differ and may involve receptor binding, endocytosis or membrane fusion. Entry is distinct from productive replication.

Professional and academic context: Reading note on the mechanisms of viral entry.

Further reading: NCBI Bookshelf · Cell Biology of Infection

Chitosan and biomaterials

Chitosan-based materials are being studied for biomedical applications. Properties depend on formulation and conditions; environmental adsorption does not establish biomedical efficacy.

Professional and academic context: This topic relates to my conceptual proposal on chitosan, which would require experimental validation.

Further reading: Recent Advances of Chitosan Formulations in Biomedical ApplicationsEvidence / context

Experimental troubleshooting

Electrophoresis: interpreting an incomplete result

Group report · CEAC FP Oficial · January 2024

Observations: Two gels were prepared: kiwi genomic DNA and purified PCR amplicons. Bands were visible in the latter, but not in the former. Possible smearing was recorded in some lanes.

Interpretation and limitations: The report discussed DNA degradation, time since extraction and preparation as possible explanations, without experimentally establishing them.

Calibration: deviations and corrections

Group report · IES Virgen de la Paloma · March 2022

Observations: Thermometer readings were compared with water melting and boiling reference points, recording temperature series and considering atmospheric pressure.

Interpretation and limitations: Deviations and correction factors were calculated. Some readings and the conclusion are inconsistent; no numeric deviation is presented as a validated calibration.

Reaction yield: evaluating losses

Individual report · IES Virgen de la Paloma · January 2023

Observations: Gas was collected, its volume measured and the yield compared with the expected amount using stoichiometric calculations.

Interpretation and limitations: Yield was incomplete. The report considered product loss, purity, side reactions and possible execution errors, without establishing a definitive cause.

Bradford: review of a poor calibration

Individual report · IES Virgen de la Paloma · February-March 2023

Observations: The report contains six albumin standards, absorbance at 595 nm and a fitted line with a reported R² of 0.4936. One milk sample was analysed.

Interpretation and limitations: Several standards deviated from the fitted line. Preparation or reading errors were considered but not confirmed. The calculated concentration is not presented as a reliable quantitative result.

Working method

From a precise question to useful, traceable scientific work.

Questions, evidence, documentation, learning and responsible decisions form one practical workflow.

01

Precise questions

Curiosity starts with observation, patience and initiative, then becomes a question that can guide study, experimental planning or technical problem-solving.

02

Traceable work

Methods, data, decisions, contributions, reviews and tests are documented so the route to a result can be examined and learned from.

03

From method to application

Study and experimental work move from method, data and analysis towards insight and practical outcomes that support health, decision-making and socially useful science.

04

Scientific integrity

Published evidence, interpretation, hypotheses and results remain separate; open questions, missing validation and technical constraints stay visible. Animal-related work is approached with welfare and 3R principles.

05

Applied learning & collaboration

Continuous learning across clinical laboratory science, biomedical research and languages supports clear communication, adaptability and work in multicultural teams.

06

Transparent AI use

I define objectives, requirements and decisions, then review, test and approve the final result. AI can support implementation, research, organisation, writing and checking.
