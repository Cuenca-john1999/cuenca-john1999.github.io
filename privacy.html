Privacy

Privacy information for professional contact.

This page explains the current data flow when the portfolio contact form is used.

Controller

Jhon M. Cuenca is responsible for the personal data submitted through this portfolio contact form. Contact is available through the professional contact form on the portfolio.

Data collected

The form asks for your name, email address and message. Web3Forms states that submission processing may also include technical metadata such as IP address, timestamp and referrer information. Please send only information needed for your professional enquiry.

Purpose and legal basis

The information is used to receive and respond to professional enquiries, laboratory opportunities, research-related contact or collaboration requests. Depending on the context, processing may be necessary for steps requested before a potential employment or other contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, or may rely on the legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR in receiving and responding to relevant professional correspondence.

Form provider and international processing

The contact form is transmitted through Web3Forms, operated by Web3Creative, which acts as processor for form submissions. Web3Forms states that it operates from India, uses infrastructure including AWS, Cloudflare and Hetzner, and may process data in multiple regions. For transfers from the EEA, United Kingdom or Switzerland that require a transfer mechanism, its DPA incorporates Standard Contractual Clauses. Its current DPA also lists spam-prevention sub-processors including CleanTalk and Akismet, which may receive the submitter IP address and email address.

Retention

The static portfolio itself does not maintain a separate submissions database. Web3Forms states that form-submission data is subject to a physical time-to-live of up to three years, while dashboard visibility can depend on the plan and data may be deleted earlier. The delivered message may also remain in the receiving email service according to its settings and policies.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may have rights including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.
